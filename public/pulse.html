How the numbers work

  • Pace: sales month to date ÷ the month's target phased evenly to today.
  • Forecast: sales month to date scaled by how the same month finished last year, so it follows the season.
  • Days of cover: available in-date stock ÷ average units sold per day over the last 28 days, per item and hub.
  • Country: the brand's country of origin, as a 3-letter code.
  • Low cover and overstock use each category's own minimum and maximum days.
  • Expiring: available stock with 30 days or less of shelf life left.
  • Margin floor: the lowest gross margin the category accepts; lines below it show under Customers & Leakage.
  • Customer pace: this month against the average of the three months before, pro-rated to today.
  • Last year, like-for-like: the current month is compared with the same days last year; earlier months with the whole month.
  • Change against last year splits into volume (more or fewer units of items sold in both years, at last year's price), price (price and discount changes on those items), new lines and lost lines (an item in a sales organisation and division sold in only one of the two years).
  • Growth contribution: the points of total growth a row adds; the rows add up to the total change in percent.
  • Margin status: below floor = under the category's margin floor; thin = within 3 points above it. Profit under floor = gross profit missing against the floor on the lines sold below it.
  • All names and figures are generated sample data.
