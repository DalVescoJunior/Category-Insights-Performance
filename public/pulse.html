How the numbers work

  • Pace: sales month to date ÷ the month's target phased evenly to today.
  • Forecast: sales month to date scaled by how the same month finished last year, so it follows the season.
  • Days of cover: available in-date stock ÷ average units sold per day over the last 28 days, per item and hub.
  • Low cover and overstock use each category's own minimum and maximum days.
  • Expiring: available stock with 30 days or less of shelf life left.
  • Margin floor: the lowest gross margin the category accepts; lines below it show under Customers & Margin.
  • Customer pace: this month against the average of the three months before, pro-rated to today.
  • All names and figures are generated sample data.
